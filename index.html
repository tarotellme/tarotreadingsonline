Understand the Cards Behind Your Reading

A tarot deck contains 78 cards, traditionally divided into the Major Arcana and Minor Arcana.

The Major Arcana includes well-known cards such as The Fool, The Magician, The High Priestess, The Empress, The Lovers, The Chariot, Strength, The Hermit, Wheel of Fortune, Justice, The Star, The Moon, The Sun, and The World.

These cards are often associated with broader life themes, major transitions, personal lessons, or significant points of reflection.

The Minor Arcana is divided into four suits:

  • Cups, often associated with emotion, relationships, and connection
  • Wands, often associated with action, creativity, motivation, and ambition
  • Swords, often associated with thought, communication, tension, and decisions
  • Pentacles, often associated with practical matters, work, resources, and material concerns

The meaning of an online tarot card can also depend on its position in a spread, the surrounding cards, the question being asked, and whether the reading uses upright and reversed tarot cards.

That is why a personalized card reading can offer more context than reading an isolated definition.

Can I get tarot readings free online?

Are online tarot readings personalized?

Tarotell.me uses your selected cards, reading format, and question to provide a personalized AI tarot interpretation rather than relying only on a single generic card definition.

What is a one-card tarot reading?

A one-card tarot reading uses a single tarot card to explore one question, theme, or point of reflection. It is one of the simplest tarot formats and can work well for daily readings and beginners.

Can tarot answer questions about love?

Love and relationship tarot readings can help you reflect on emotions, communication, relationship patterns, attraction, uncertainty, and your own perspective. They should not be treated as proof of another person's private thoughts or feelings.

Can I ask tarot about career or money?

Yes. You can use career and money tarot readings for reflection around work, goals, priorities, choices, or concerns. Tarot is not a replacement for professional financial, legal, career, or employment advice.

What is the difference between Major Arcana and Minor Arcana?

The Major Arcana contains cards commonly linked with larger life themes and significant experiences. The Minor Arcana contains four suits, Cups, Wands, Swords, and Pentacles, that often relate to everyday emotions, actions, thoughts, relationships, work, and practical matters.
